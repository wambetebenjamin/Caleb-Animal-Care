Birds hide illness until they are very sick — in the wild, a visible limp is an invitation. By the time a parrot fluffs up in the corner of the cage, you are late. Prevention and daily observation are everything.

## The Njoroge essentials

- **Diet beyond seeds:** seed-only diets cause fatty liver disease in months. Build the bowl around pellets, vegetables (sukuma wiki, carrot, pumpkin, dhania) and a little fruit. Avocado and chocolate are toxic — never share them.
- **Cuttlebone and mineral block:** constant access, especially for laying hens.
- **UV light:** an hour of safe, unfiltered morning sun keeps vitamin D3 and feathers healthy.
- **Cage maths:** the bird must fully spread both wings without touching bars; two perches of different diameters prevent bumblefoot.

## Boredom kills

Greys are as bright as a four-year-old child. Rotate chew toys weekly, hide food in foraging toys, and give ten minutes of out-of-cage flight time in a closed room daily. Feather-plucking is usually stress, not mites.

## When to call the vet

Fluffed feathers beyond a nap, tail-bobbing breathing, droppings changing colour for more than a day, or sitting on the cage floor. Birds need a vet who reads avian smears — book our exotic slots with Dr. Njoroge.
